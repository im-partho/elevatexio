ELEVATEXIO Program — Study in India Program

About ElevateX.Io

ELEVATEXIO is an education and career-focused platform dedicated to bridging the gap between learning and employment by helping international students access quality higher education opportunities in India, scholarship opportunities, university admissions, internships, employability programs, and personalized career guidance. Our approach goes beyond traditional admission assistance by combining education, skills development, industry exposure, real-world experience, and career support to help students turn academic opportunities into meaningful professional outcomes. Through our three core verticals—ELEVATEX Careers, ELEVATEX Digital, and ELEVATEX IT Solutions—we create a connected ecosystem where education, technology, innovation, and professional growth come together. With a mission to transform education into opportunity, ELEVATEXIO empowers students to build relevant skills, gain industry exposure, and confidently shape successful careers in an increasingly global world.

We operate across three verticals: ElevateX Careers, ElevateX Digital, and ElevateX IT Solutions.
